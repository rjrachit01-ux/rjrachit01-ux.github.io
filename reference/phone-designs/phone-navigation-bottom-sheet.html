What to build

  • Closed (panels 1 and 3): the logo on the left and a menu button (three lines) on the right, with a tap area of at least 44 × 44px. No separate "Get in touch" button in the bar.
  • Open (panel 2): the page behind dims. The bar stays visible and the menu button becomes an X. A panel slides up from the bottom (about 0.35s, cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1)) with rounded top corners (20px) and a small handle (38px wide, 4px tall). Four rows, each 56px tall with a 1px divider: My work, About me, Playground, Visitor gallery. The current page has a small terracotta dot on the right. A full-width dark "Get in touch" block, 52px tall and fully rounded (26px), sits 18px below the last row.
  • Bar colours — existing rules, unchanged: transparent with white text over the dark top of the page (panel 1). Light paper background with dark text once "Some Things I made" reaches the nav (panel 3).
  • Closing: tap the X, tap the dimmed area, tap a link, or press Escape.
  • Accessibility: the button is named "Menu" and reports open or closed. Focus moves into the panel when it opens and returns to the button when it closes. No animation if "reduced motion" is on.
