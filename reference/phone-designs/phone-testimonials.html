Testimonial cards on phones

The chosen phone version. It uses the site's real card styles and all eight real testimonials, and it is live: it moves by itself and you can use the arrows. This file shows only the chosen version at the real phone width of 390px. Phone widths only. Laptops and tablets don't change.

What to build

  • Width: the screen width minus 36px each side (318px on a 390px phone), instead of the fixed 340px.
  • Centred: the front card is centred, including the first card. The neighbouring cards show at the edges, tilted and faded, as on the laptop.
  • Height: the cards grow to fit their text, all to the same height (the tallest card's), never less than today's 420px. No quote may be cut off. On the laptop the 340px card keeps its fixed height. Rachit is trimming Emeka's quote himself.
  • The sliding calculation on the site has the card width and gap built in (340 + 20). On phones it must use the real card width, and centre the front card. The side space around the carousel (60px each side) is removed on phones. See the script at the bottom of this file for a working version.
  • Space: 32px from the heading box to the cards, and 80px above and below the section, as in the spacing standard.
  • Unchanged: the look of the cards (colours, accent bar, fonts, tick), the auto-advance every 2 seconds, and pause while the mouse is over the cards or arrows.
  • Not decided yet: touch behaviour (swipe left or right, and tap to pause). Ask Rachit before building it.
