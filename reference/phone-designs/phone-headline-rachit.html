RACHIT headline on phones — one row, the word underneath

The chosen phone version of the headline. It is live. Tap the letters. This file shows only the chosen version at the real phone width of 390px. Phone widths only (up to 640px). The laptop version doesn't change.

What to build

  • The letters: all six sit in one row across the full screen width, with a 16px margin each side (text size about 92px on a 390px phone). They must scale so all six fit on every phone width from about 360px up. They keep the laptop look: cream with a terracotta offset shadow, and the active letter in terracotta with a soft light shadow.
  • The word: underneath the row, one at a time, about 30px below it, at about 36px. Its first letter is cream and bold, and the rest is terracotta.
  • Behaviour: it cycles through the six words by itself every 1.4 seconds, as on the laptop. Tapping a letter shows that word and holds it for 4 seconds, then the cycle carries on. Each letter needs a tap area at least 44px wide, including the narrow "I".
  • The sticker says "Tap a letter!" on phones. On the laptop it still says "Hover over!".
  • The line at the bottom wraps onto two lines, centred.
  • Accessibility: the headline keeps its full-sentence label. The letters are buttons with clear names. If "reduced motion" is on, the automatic cycle stays off and a tap is the only way to change the word.
